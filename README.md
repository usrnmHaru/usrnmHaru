## Projects

### 🚨 [NagaRescue](https://github.com/NagaRescue) — Emergency Response & Disaster Management
`Lead Developer`

A seven-repo platform connecting residents, barangay officials, the city command center, rescue units, and evacuation managers across **27 barangays**. An SOS moves from resident to barangay operator to ComCen dispatch to responder as one tracked request.

Built for the conditions it's meant to survive: SMS fallback when the network drops, offline maps with barangay boundaries bundled rather than fetched, and early-warning broadcasts on a dedicated alarm channel.

`React Native` `Expo` `React` `Vite` `TypeScript` `Express` `PostgreSQL`
